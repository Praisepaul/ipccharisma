Welcome to the IPC Charisma Church

IPC Charisma Church (formerly known as Charismatic Church) was founded in the year 2011 in Ganganadu, a village in Udupi district, in the state of Karnataka, in India. It initially started as a prayer group by Pastor.Paulson Valookkaran Augusthy with his family. As time passed by, people joined the group. The church grew slowly and the fame spreaded. On April,2011 IPC Charisma Church conducted its first public services. Founded as a nondenominational church, its emphasis was on the basics of Christianity.Worship services were conducted in a small hall at Pastor. Paulson's house. People from many different areas started contacting. In 2012, services were moved to the newly built chapel at the present location on Ganganadu. Less than a month later, two services were being conducted on Sunday mornings, the first was the Sunday school classes, and Sunday Services. The church became known as "the fastest growing and the spiritual church in the towns nearby".

Ten new churches were established under Pr. Paulson's leadership, one in Hemady and the other in Thudali. Hemady is a village near to Kundapura and is 45kms far from the home church. Thudali is another village near to Shiroor and is nearly 40kms from the home church. Other churches were established in other parts of India including Uttar Pradesh,Punjab,Gujarat. A total of 100 believers are part of this Church. Also a huge number of people are viritually a part of the church, all over the world. More important than numbers, programs, and structures, however, is the foundation for the spiritual life of the church that has been built.

This foundation includes sound doctrine, spiritual leadership, and active service. We are convinced that God's legacy of faithfulness to us will continue in the future if we remain faithful to Him and His Word and are lead by his Spirit.

Pastor Paulson's Testimony

From Darkness to His Wonderful Light

Isaiah 53:6 says, "We all, like sheep, have gone astray, each of us has turned to his own way." But the Son of Man who came to seek and to save the lost

found me and saved me. I am very conscious that I did not choose Him, but He chose me. years ago, on a day like this, our wonderful Saviour called me out of darkness into His wonderful light to declare His praises. Thus, what the Apostle Paul says in Romans 10:20 has become a literal reality in my life, i.e., God would be found by people who weren't looking for Him.

It was around midnight, May 28, 1993. The Spirit of God put real pressure on me and told me three times to be His witness. Finally, I yielded to that pressure, and my peace was great. The Spirit of God said, "Go home to your family and tell them how much the Lord has done for you and how merciful He has been." (Mark 5:19) "So come and listen, all you who fear God; let me tell you what He has done for me." (Ps. 66:16) The Lord pardoned my sins and healed my sickness. Thus, He turned my wailing into dancing. He removed my sackcloth and clothed me with joy, that my heart may sing to my Lord and not be silent. The joy of this salvation is great. Hallelujah!

On May 31, 1968, I was born into a Catholic family in Ernakulam Dist. in Kerala. I was the 2nd in a family of 7 children. My parents called me Paulachen, and that name is still retained. My father was a well-to-do person. He was respected in society and in the church. Besides, he was the choirmaster in the church. We led a comfortable life.

All of a sudden, we had a severe blow. My father had an illegitimate relationship with a helper woman in our home and had a child from this alliance. He was disgraced and gradually turned to drinking. Because of this woman, my father became a Hindu. He started practising sorcery. He became mentally ill and was being treated. He ill-treated my mother and sold the family wealth. We were in great poverty. I started working in various homes. My brothers and sisters were sent to orphanages.

A bitter hatred and a sense of revenge grew in my young heart. During my final year in high school, I left home in search of a means of living. I worked in hotels. Meanwhile, I became addicted to smoking and drugs. I was leading an immoral life. I had no peace or happiness. I committed petty thefts and watched video movies. The villains in them influenced me. My heart hungered to play that role.

One night, I hit my father on his head and killed him. Then I killed that woman also in the same manner. Police caught me. At the age of 19, I was behind bars. When I came out on bail, I committed some theft and, during that course of action, I fatally wounded some people. So I was given two life-sentences plus 11 years as punishment and sent to Central Jail, Kannur. I had no hope. There was no way to come out alive. My people hated me. But I had a mother who diligently prayed for me.

My heart was like a stormy sea. Waves of frustration played tricks upon my mind. I was afraid for my mental equilibrium. At times, my heart was full of remorse. Sad to say, I tasted the brutal strength of the police on every part of my body. I used to vomit blood-tainted sputum. I was an asthma patient too. So I was moved to a special part of the jail. I wanted to end my life. But merciful God started to work in me.

It so happened then that a charismatic Prayer Group visited the jail. They sang and preached the love of Jesus. I was attracted towards these meetings. They sang in Malayalam like this:

God's love is eternal; it has no boundaries,
It is immeasurable love,
Even if a mother forgets her child,
The Shepherd who watches the sheep would look for you.

It was a beautiful promise. It touched my heart. I started to weep. This promise found me when I was considering ending my life; this Eternal Love came in search of me. "Unless the Lord had given me help, I would soon have dwelt in the silence of death." (Ps. 94:17)

Then, for the first time, I was on parole for 10 days. Straight away, I went to Divine Center, Muringoor. On Tuesday, May 25, 1993, I was listening to the message. I was strongly convicted about my sinful condition. The priest emphasized that we are God's temple and that God's Spirit lives in us. He continued, "If anyone defiles and spoils God's temple, God will destroy him. For God's temple is holy and clean and you are that temple." (1 Cor. 3:16, 17; 6:19, 20) He admonished us to give up sinful living. I wept in my helplessness. A great power like electricity passed through my entire system. I repented of my sins. I wept especially for the murders I committed. I asked Jesus to forgive me. Isaiah 59:12 gave me great hope. Again, I cried out to Jesus, "Forgive me and save me." Something happened within me.

Jesus accepted my broken heart. He put His hand on me and said, "Son, you are mine. I need you." Blessed moments. I remember it afresh even today. I surrendered myself before the love of Jesus. I sensed that I was healed. It has been years since then. I never had an asthmatic attack. Thus, I went back forgiven and healed.

It did not end there (2 Cor. 5:17). Jesus filled my heart with heavenly love. I am at peace with all. Wherever needed, I asked for forgiveness. My thoughts, speech, character—everything changed. I have a great desire to read the Bible and pray. It gives me immense pleasure.

My heart was filled with gratitude for my Saviour. One night, I dozed off to sleep. But I had my awareness deep inside me. Soon, a bright light fell over me. I was so much like a burning coal. Angels stood around me singing praises in a special language. My heart was burning. It is difficult to explain this. I was anointed with the Holy Spirit and was given the gift of speaking in tongues. Then I woke up—others did not realise anything.

I know that I am saved by God's grace. I should never be proud. The Holy Spirit continues to instruct me and leads me in all truth. The Spirit of God put a burden in my heart for the other inmates. A prayer fellowship was started, and I shared my testimony with them. Many were saved. As jail inmates were transferred from one jail to another, this fellowship took another dimension. There is a very strong group of believers in these jails.

Meanwhile, I happened to read some books written by Bro. P. G. Varghis. They greatly influenced my spiritual life. In the year 1995, I met Pastor Roy Cherian from Ernakulam. He further instructed me on Bible truth. Then, according to the Word of God, I took water baptism. Jesus made me free from sin and the influence of wrong teaching. I learned the truth, and now I worship God in Spirit and truth. I John 3:18, Luke 4:18, Galatians 5:14. The Spirit of God opened my eyes to accept this wonderful freedom.

After serving 11 years of prison sentence, I was released on July 2, 1999. A few months before I got my release, in March 1999, I married my beautiful wife, Mariamma Paulson. Then God gave me two blessed children, Grace Valookkaran Paul and Praise Paul, in 2000 and 2002. We then moved into a rural village in Karnataka called Byndoor, in the district of Udupi, in 2007 after receiving a vision from God. We have been serving the Lord here since then for the past years, according to the grace we have received. Our daughter Grace, a Speech-Language Pathologist, is now married to a blessed child of God, Jeffin Anto Thomas, a Software Engineer, and they are living in part of Bangalore and serving the Lord in their own ways along with their jobs. My son Praise is working as an Engineer in Pune. We have been able to go through many ups and downs in life, various moments of spiritual warfare, times when our faith was tested, but God has been gracious and merciful to us in all of these.

When I look back, I can only borrow the Apostle Paul's words from Philippians 1:12: "Now I want you to know, brothers, that what has happened to me has really served to advance the gospel."

Kindly keep me and my family in your prayer.

Your brother in Christ Jesus,
Pr. Paulson V A
